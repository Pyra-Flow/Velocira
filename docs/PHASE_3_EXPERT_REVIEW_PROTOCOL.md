# Phase 3 expert review protocol

The Phase 3 implementation test verifies state, evidence history, readiness, and access control. It does not replace a requirements-engineering expert. Use this protocol to complete the remaining human definition of done.

## Ten representative briefs

Review one completed interview for each scenario:

1. Appointment booking for a small clinic.
2. Student learning-management portal.
3. Marketplace for local service providers.
4. Fleet maintenance tracking system.
5. Internal employee onboarding workflow.
6. Mobile food-delivery application.
7. Inventory and purchase-order API.
8. IoT energy-monitoring dashboard.
9. AI-assisted customer-support triage tool.
10. Regulated financial expense-approval workflow.

## Reviewer checklist

For each brief, record the reviewer, date, scenario, and outcome. Confirm that:

- The question order was relevant and did not force unnecessary repetition.
- Problem, users, scope/exclusions, workflow, data/integrations, quality, constraints, and risks are either evidenced or visibly marked unknown.
- Assumptions and open questions are understandable and trace back to an answer or deterministic rule.
- No user answer was converted into an invented business rule.
- Any contradiction is visible, correctly categorized, and blocks generation where material.
- The canonical brief is a useful, faithful summary and edits show their downstream impact.

Do not mark a scenario passed until a qualified reviewer records the result in the project or an approved review log.
